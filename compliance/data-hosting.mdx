All Hirify data — candidate profiles, interview recordings, transcripts, and enriched fields — is stored exclusively in France on sovereign, ISO 27001-certified infrastructure. This means your recruitment data never leaves French territory as part of core platform operations, giving you a clear and auditable data residency posture for compliance, enterprise procurement, and client assurance purposes.

## Infrastructure Details

The table below lists the infrastructure components that power Hirify's platform and where each element is hosted.

| Component | Provider | Location | Certification |
|---|---|---|---|
| Application Hosting | Scalingo | Paris, France | ISO 27001 |
| File Storage | Scaleway | France | ISO 27001 |
| AI Analysis | Mistral | France | — |
| Transcription | Gladia | France | — |

All core data — including candidate profiles, interview audio, transcripts, and AI-generated enrichments — resides within Scalingo and Scaleway's French infrastructure. AI processing by Mistral and audio transcription by Gladia are performed within France and do not result in persistent storage of candidate data outside the Scalingo/Scaleway perimeter.

---

## What 'Data Hosted in France' Means

Choosing infrastructure hosted entirely in France has concrete legal and operational implications for your recruitment team:

- **Your candidate data is subject to French and EU law only.** Data stored in France is governed by the GDPR and French data protection law (Loi Informatique et Libertés). It is not subject to the jurisdiction of non-EU governments.
- **No US Cloud Act exposure.** Because Hirify does not rely on US-based infrastructure providers for core data storage or processing, your candidate records are not reachable under the US CLOUD Act. This is a meaningful distinction for public sector clients and organisations with strict data sovereignty requirements.
- **Auditable data residency.** If a client, auditor, or regulator asks where candidate data is stored, the answer is unambiguous: France. Hirify can provide written confirmation of data residency for enterprise and public sector procurement processes.

## Encryption

Hirify applies encryption at every layer of the data lifecycle:

- **Data in transit** — all communication between your browser, the Hirify application, and backend services is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher. Unencrypted HTTP connections are rejected.
- **Data at rest** — all stored data, including interview recordings, transcripts, and candidate profile fields, is encrypted at rest using AES-256 encryption.
- **Encryption key management** — encryption keys are managed within the French infrastructure perimeter. Keys are never stored or managed by US-based services.
