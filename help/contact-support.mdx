Hirify's support team is based in France and available to help you with every stage of your setup — from connecting your ATS for the first time to answering compliance questions, troubleshooting integrations, and resolving technical issues. Choose the channel below that best matches your question and plan.

## Support Channels

## Before You Contact Us

Many common questions are already answered in the documentation. Checking these resources first will often save you time:

- **General questions:** Visit the [FAQ](/help/faq) for answers to the most common questions about Hirify, plans, compliance, and integrations.
- **ATS connection problems:** See [Troubleshoot ATS Connection Problems](/help/ats-connection-issues) for step-by-step guidance on authorisation errors, import delays, field mapping, and sync issues.
- **Recording and transcription problems:** See [Troubleshoot Interview Recording and Transcription](/help/interview-recording) for help with missed recordings, missing transcripts, and enrichment failures.
- **GDPR and AI Act:** Visit the [GDPR compliance page](/compliance/gdpr) and the [EU AI Act compliance page](/compliance/ai-act) for detailed information about how Hirify handles candidate data.

## What to Include in Your Support Request

Providing the right information upfront helps the support team diagnose and resolve your issue without back-and-forth delays. When you contact support, include as many of the following as are relevant:

- **Your ATS provider name** — for example, Bullhorn, Teamtailor, or Boondmanager
- **Your Hirify plan** — Starter, Hub, or Volume
- **Steps to reproduce the issue** — what you did, what you expected to happen, and what happened instead
- **Any error messages you saw** — copy the full error text, including any error codes
- **Interview ID** — if your issue relates to a specific interview, include the ID from the URL in **Hirify > Interviews**
- **Time the issue first occurred** — this helps the team correlate your report with server logs

<Tip>
  Hub and Volume plan customers receive **priority support**, with faster response times and escalation paths for critical issues. Volume plan customers are also assigned a **dedicated onboarding contact** for the first 30 days after activation, available to guide your team through ATS setup, template configuration, and initial candidate imports. If you are onboarding a large team and have not heard from your onboarding contact within 24 hours of activation, email [support@hirify.fr](mailto:support@hirify.fr) to be connected.
</Tip>
