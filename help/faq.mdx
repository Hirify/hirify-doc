Hirify is designed to fit naturally into your existing recruitment workflow — no migrations, no disruption, just smarter hiring. Whether you're evaluating Hirify for the first time or troubleshooting a specific question, the answers below cover the topics recruiters ask about most.

<Accordion title="What is Hirify?">
  Hirify is an AI-powered HR Hub built for recruitment teams. It connects to your existing ATS, captures and transcribes interviews automatically, and enriches candidate profiles with structured data from each conversation. Your entire talent pool becomes searchable through natural language, so you can find the right candidate in seconds — not hours.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do I need to migrate my ATS to use Hirify?">
  No. Hirify integrates directly with your existing ATS using a two-way connection. Your current workflows, pipelines, and candidate data stay exactly where they are. Hirify enriches and synchronises data without requiring any migration or re-entry of information.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Which ATS platforms does Hirify support?">
  Hirify supports a wide range of leading French and international ATS platforms. For a complete and up-to-date list of supported integrations, see the [ATS Integrations overview](/integrations/ats-overview). If your ATS is not listed, contact [support@hirify.fr](mailto:support@hirify.fr) to request an integration.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is my data stored in France?">
  Yes. All data processed and stored by Hirify is hosted exclusively on French infrastructure through Scalingo, located in Paris. Scalingo holds ISO 27001 certification, ensuring rigorous information security management. Your candidate data never leaves French territory.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is Hirify GDPR compliant?">
  Yes. Hirify is designed from the ground up to comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). This includes lawful basis for processing, data minimisation, candidate rights management, and data retention controls. For full details, see the [GDPR compliance page](/compliance/gdpr).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does Hirify comply with the EU AI Act?">
  Yes. Hirify's AI features are built around the principles of transparency and explainability. The system does not make autonomous hiring decisions — all AI-generated insights are presented as supporting information for your recruiters to evaluate. For more information, see the [EU AI Act compliance page](/compliance/ai-act).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do I have to inform candidates that their interview is being recorded?">
  Yes, and this is a legal requirement under GDPR. You must obtain explicit, informed consent from every candidate before recording begins. Hirify provides built-in tools to help you manage consent, but it is your responsibility as a data controller to ensure candidates are informed. See the [GDPR compliance page](/compliance/gdpr) for guidance on consent collection and what to do if a candidate withdraws consent.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is the difference between the Starter and Hub plans?">
  The **Starter plan** (from €24/month billed annually, or €29/month billed monthly) gives you Hirify's core interview capture and transcription features, and works as a standalone tool without any ATS connection. The **Hub plan** (from €49/month billed annually, or €59/month billed monthly) adds full two-way ATS integration and access to the Talent Pool Chat, which lets you search your entire enriched candidate pool using natural language. If you work with an ATS and want searchable talent pools, Hub is the right choice.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I use Hirify without an ATS?">
  Yes. The Starter plan is designed to work as a standalone interview intelligence tool. You can capture, transcribe, and enrich interviews without connecting any ATS. If you later decide to connect your ATS, you can upgrade to the Hub plan at any time from Settings > Billing.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I cancel my subscription?">
  You can cancel your subscription at any time from **Settings > Billing > Cancel Subscription**. Your account remains active until the end of the current billing period. After cancellation, your data is retained for 30 days, during which you can export it. After 30 days, all data is permanently deleted in accordance with our data retention policy. If you need help before cancelling, contact [support@hirify.fr](mailto:support@hirify.fr).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What languages does Hirify support for transcription?">
  Hirify's transcription engine is optimised for **French and English**, covering the vast majority of interviews conducted by French recruitment teams and international ESNs. If you regularly conduct interviews in other languages and need transcription support, contact [support@hirify.fr](mailto:support@hirify.fr) to discuss your requirements.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How long does an ATS talent pool import take?">
  Most imports complete within **5 to 30 minutes**, depending on the size of your talent pool and the responsiveness of your ATS. Large pools containing 10,000 or more candidates may take up to 30 minutes. You can monitor import progress in **Settings > Integrations**. If your import has not completed after one hour, contact support with your ATS provider name and account details.
</Accordion>
