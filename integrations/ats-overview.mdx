Hirify integrates with the leading ATS platforms used by French and international recruitment teams. The integration is two-way — Hirify reads your existing talent pool on first connection and writes enriched candidate data back after every interview, so your ATS always reflects the latest structured insights your team has captured.

## Integration Capabilities

Every ATS connection Hirify establishes supports the following capabilities out of the box:

| Capability | Description |
|---|---|
| **Profile Import** | All existing candidate profiles are imported on first connection, giving Hirify immediate access to your full talent pool. |
| **Real-time Sync** | New candidates added to your ATS and updates to existing records sync automatically — no manual exports required. |
| **Enrichment Write-back** | Structured fields captured during interviews — skills, mobility, salary expectations, and availability — are written directly to candidate profiles in your ATS. |
| **Interview Linking** | Each enrichment entry is linked to the specific interview that generated it, preserving full traceability for compliance and audit purposes. |

## Supported ATS Platforms

Hirify supports leading French and international ATS platforms. Because the list of supported systems grows regularly as the team evaluates and certifies new integrations, the definitive list is maintained inside the application itself.

To see which platforms are available for your account:

1. Log in at [app.hirify.fr](https://app.hirify.fr)
2. Navigate to **Settings > Integrations**
3. Browse the list of available platforms — each one shows its current connection status and supported capabilities

If you'd prefer guidance before logging in, reach out to [support@hirify.fr](mailto:support@hirify.fr) and the team can confirm compatibility with your specific ATS.

<Info>
  Don't see your ATS listed? Contact [support@hirify.fr](mailto:support@hirify.fr) — the team evaluates new integrations regularly and can advise on timelines for platforms under consideration.
</Info>

<Note>
  ATS integration is available on the **Hub** and **Volume** plans. If you're on the Starter plan, you can still use Hirify as a fully featured standalone interview and talent pool tool — enriched profiles are stored within Hirify, but they won't sync back to an external ATS.
</Note>
