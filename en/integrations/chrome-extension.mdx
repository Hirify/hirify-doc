The extension opens Hirify in a side panel of your browser. You start an interview and query your talent pool without switching tabs.

## Install the extension

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the card from the dashboard">
    On the **Dashboard**, in the **Go further** section, the **Hirify in your browser** card reads: "Record and transcribe your interviews straight from Chrome, without leaving your ATS." Click **Install the extension**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the extension to Chrome">
    The Chrome Web Store opens in a new tab, on the Hirify listing. Start the installation from that page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pin the icon">
    Once the extension is installed, pin its icon to the Chrome toolbar. It opens and closes the side panel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Connect the extension to your account

<Note>
  The extension panel is displayed in French. Its labels are given below as they appear, with their meaning in parentheses.
</Note>

The first time you open it, the panel shows a **Connexion** (sign in) screen. You can take one of two paths:

- **Se connecter avec Hirify** (sign in with Hirify): the shortest. Your browser sends you to your Hirify workspace to sign in, then the panel opens on your account.
- **Options avancées** (advanced options): expand it at the bottom of the screen if the first path fails (expired session, restrictive browser). Then sign in with an access token.

## Sign in with an access token

The token is a personal key created in Hirify that identifies you to the extension.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the token in Hirify">
    Open **Settings**, then **Security**. The **Access tokens** section says: "Create a personal token to connect the browser extension or the API to your account. The token is shown only once." Click **Create a token**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Give it a name that means something to you, such as "Chrome extension". Confirm with **Create**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the value shown">
    The **Your new token** window shows the key only once. Copy it, then click **Done**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it into the panel">
    In the extension, under **Options avancées**, paste the key into the **Jeton d'accès** (access token) field and click **Se connecter par jeton** (sign in with token).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  A token gives access to your account. Don't share it and don't paste it into a shared document. If in doubt, revoke it: in **Settings**, **Security**, click the delete icon next to the token, then **Revoke**. Any application using it loses access immediately.
</Warning>

## What you can do from the panel

Once you're signed in, the panel offers two tabs.

- **Assistant**: ask your talent pool questions, just as in the app. See [Ask a question](/en/assistant/ask-a-question).
- **Entretiens** (interviews): find your recent interviews and open one to review it.

The **Démarrer** (start) button starts a new interview without going back to the app. You choose between **Visioconférence** (video meeting), **Entretien en présentiel** (in-person interview), and **Appel téléphonique** (phone call). The three channels and what each one requires are described in [Choose how to record](/en/interviews/choose-how-to-record).

On a SmartRecruiters candidate profile, the panel shows the matching Hirify profile at the top when there is one.

## Good to know

- The extension is installed from the Chrome Web Store. It isn't distributed as a file.
- If your plan doesn't include phone calls, the option stays visible in the creation menu but is inactive, marked "indisponible" (unavailable). See [Make a call](/en/calling/make-a-call).
- Matching with a candidate profile currently only works on SmartRecruiters pages. Other ATSs don't show this banner.
- The extension doesn't replace the app. Templates, analysis, Sourcing, and Analytics stay in Hirify.
- The access token is shown only once. If you lose it, it can't be recovered. Create a new one and revoke the old one.
- Installing the extension doesn't exempt you from informing the candidate before any recording.
