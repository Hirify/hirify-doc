Every board Hirify builds for you is saved automatically, as private. There's no Save button.

## Find your boards

In the sidebar, expand **Analytics** and click **Boards**. The page lists the boards you can access: yours and those your colleagues shared. Each row shows the author, the age, and the visibility, **Private** or **Team**.

A search field and a sort, **Recent** or **Alphabetical**, help you find your way. Pinned boards stay on top whatever the sort. The **Build** button leads to the page where boards are created.

A ready-made board opened from **Build** only appears in the list after a click on **Add to my boards**.

## Evolve a board with Hirify

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the board">
    Under its title, a line shows its visibility, its author, and when it was last saved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Ask Hirify">
    The button, at the top right, opens the conversation in a panel next to the board. It picks up the conversation that built the board.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask a question or ask for a change">
    A question ("why this delay?") gets an answer and the board stays as it is. A change ("add the split by recruiter") updates the board in place, without changing its name or its sharing. The block added or changed carries the **New** label.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Close Hirify** closes the panel.

<Note>
  On a colleague's shared board, a change creates your own copy, which opens. Your colleague's board isn't touched.
</Note>

## Change the period of a block

Some blocks, such as interviews by weekday, offer their own period in their header: **1W**, **1M**, **3M**, **6M**, or **1Y**. Only that block is recalculated. This choice isn't saved and goes away when the page reloads.

To change the period of the whole board, use the **Period** chip above the blocks.

## Get the board analyzed

The **Analyze this dashboard** button asks for a reading of the figures shown. While it runs, it shows **Analyzing…**.

The analysis covers the current view, filters included. Observations show in the **What Hirify notices** panel, and those about a specific block also sit behind that block's info marker. Each observation offers **Ask Hirify**, which opens the conversation with a question about that point already written.

A line sums up the result: **Analysis · 1 observation**, **Analysis · N observations**, or **Analysis · nothing to report**. The button then becomes **Run analysis again**.

The refresh button recalculates the figures. The **Refreshed at** mention gives the time of the last calculation.

## Set a target on a metric

A target goes on a block that shows a single number, on a board you own.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the target icon">
    It appears in the block's header, with the tooltip **Set a target**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the value">
    The **Block target** window asks for a **Target value**. The block's unit shows to the right of the field. Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The block then shows the target value. When the measure has a known direction of improvement, its color shows whether the target is met. To change or clear the target, open the icon again, now with the tooltip **Edit target**, then click **Save** or **Remove**.

## Share, pin, rename, duplicate, delete

These actions are in the actions menu (the **…** button) on a board's page. The menu at the end of each row of the list offers them too, except **Arrange blocks**.

- **Share with your team** makes the board visible to your organization's administrators and managers. **Make private** undoes it. Opened by a colleague, a shared board shows **Shared by** followed by your name.
- **Pin** moves the board to the top of the list. **Unpin** undoes it.
- **Rename** opens the **Rename dashboard** window.
- **Duplicate** creates a private copy that you own.
- **Arrange blocks** lets you drag a block by its handle, set its size to **Small**, **Medium**, or **Full width**, rename it, or delete it. Nothing is saved until **Done**.
- **Delete** asks for confirmation: "This can't be undone."

<Warning>
  Deleting a board is permanent. A shared board disappears for your colleagues too.
</Warning>

## Good to know

- Only a board's owner can rename it, pin it, change its visibility, set a target, arrange its blocks, or delete it. Others can read it, ask a question about it, and duplicate it.
- A board saves a composition, not figures. The data is recalculated each time it opens.
- Filters and the period aren't saved in the board. They live in the page address, which makes them shareable but not persistent.
- "Your team" means your organization's administrators and managers, the only people with access to Analytics.
