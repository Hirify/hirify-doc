</Note>

Hirify can assign you a dedicated phone line. You call your candidates from the app, and the conversation is transcribed like an interview. Getting a line takes two steps: verifying your company, then choosing the number.

## Why company verification is required

Assigning a business number is regulated. Before a line is assigned to you, your company's existence and the address of its registered office must be verified. The screen explains that this verification gets you a dedicated business number for your calls, that it's a regulatory requirement, and that your information is transmitted securely.

Until the file is approved, number selection stays closed. The page then shows "The Hirify line depends on business verification."

## Put together the company file

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Calling page">
    Click **Settings**, at the bottom of the sidebar, then **Calling** in the left-hand panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your company details">
    The **Business verification** section asks for **Company name**, **SIRET / SIREN**, **Address**, **City**, **Postal code**, **Contact**, and **Contact email**. These fields are required. **Address line 2** and **Contact phone** are optional. The **Country** field can't be changed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Flag anything unusual">
    The **Additional notes** field accepts 2,000 characters. Use it if a document is in a name other than the company's, or if a move is under way. The message is sent along with the file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save draft**. You can come back and finish the file later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Upload your documents

The **Required documents** block shows a counter such as "1 / 2 required". Two documents are expected.

- **Registration extract or ID**: a company registration extract (Kbis) under 3 months old, or the legal representative's ID.
- **Proof of registered office**: a utility bill (telecom, energy, internet) under 3 months old in the company's name, or a commercial lease.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the document type">
    Select the kind of document from the drop-down list: **Legal representative's ID**, **Company registration extract (under 3 months old)**, or **Utility bill or commercial lease (under 3 months old)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload the file">
    Click **Add a document**. The screen specifies "Accepted formats: PDF, JPG, PNG. Max size: 10 MB." To remove a file uploaded by mistake, click the cross at the right of its line.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit the file">
    Once the required fields are filled in and both documents are uploaded, the **Submit for verification** button appears at the bottom of the form.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  The proof of registered office must show your company's exact name. A personal bill, or one in the company's former name, will be rejected unless you explained it in **Additional notes**.
</Warning>

## Follow the review and answer the verification team

After you submit, the page switches to **File submitted** and shows "Verification in progress". The screen gives a review time of "1 to 3 business days on average". The **Refresh** button updates the file's status on demand.

The **Messages with our verification team** block opens a thread attached to your file. Use it to clear up a doubt before it turns into a rejection. Unread messages are flagged with a **New** badge and a counter in the block title. The reply field accepts 2,000 characters. Click **Send**. The screen states that the verification team "usually replies within 24 hours on business days".

If the file is rejected, the **Reason for rejection** shows at the top of the section. The **Edit information** button reopens the form. Fix what's wrong, replace the document if needed, then submit the file again.

## Get your Hirify number

As soon as the file is approved, the page shows "Pick your number" and opens the **Hirify number** section.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Filter the search">
    The drop-down list offers **All regions** or a regional area code. The **Ends with** field lets you set the last digits of the number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search">
    Click **Search**. Each result shows the number and its locality. If nothing matches, the page says "No number available with these criteria." Broaden your criteria.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reserve the number">
    Click **Select** on the line you want. The screen switches to "Activating your line" and updates on its own. Activation can take a few minutes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once the line is active, the number shows at the top of the page with "Dedicated line for your organization" and the badges **Active**, the locality, then **Direct** or **Forwarding on**.

{/* SCREENSHOT: the Calling page in its active state, with the number in large type and the badges. */}

## Forward incoming calls to another number

The **Inbound forwarding** section redirects calls received on your line to another phone. Turn on **Enable forwarding**, then enter the **Forwarding number** in the field that appears. The line's badge changes from **Direct** to **Forwarding on**.

## Release the line

When you no longer need the line, expand **Advanced settings** at the bottom of the page. The **Release the line** section warns "The number is released for good and can't be reused." Click **Release**, then confirm with **Yes, release this line**.

<Warning>
  Releasing a line can't be undone. The number will no longer be available in Hirify, and you'll have to choose a new one to get a dedicated line again.
</Warning>

## Who can do this

The verification file is reserved for the organization's administrators. Only they fill it in, upload the documents, submit it, and reply in the thread. A member who opens the page sees "Admins only". In the thread, they read "Only an admin can reply from here." Once the organization is verified, any member of the organization can choose the number, set up inbound forwarding, and release the line.

## Good to know

- The **Calling** entry only appears in **Settings** if your plan includes calling.
- The page manages only one dedicated line per user at a time. The verification file covers the whole organization.
- The file's country is set to France, and the numbers offered are French.
- If assignment fails, the page shows "The previous activation didn't go through." Run a new search to pick another number.
- Hirify never sends messages to a candidate from this line. It's for making and receiving calls.
