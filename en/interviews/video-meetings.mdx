The meeting assistant joins your video meeting, records it, and transcribes it. It shows up in the participant list like any other guest.

## Three ways to bring in the meeting assistant

| Path | When to use it | What you need |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paste the link on the Dashboard | The meeting starts now or in a few minutes | The video meeting link |
| Invite the assistant's address in the calendar invitation | The interview is already scheduled | Nothing else |
| Add the assistant from the **Calendar** | You also want to choose the template for the interview notes | A connected calendar |

All three lead to the same result: an interview that appears under **Interviews** and goes through the same processing.

## Paste a Meet or Teams link from the Dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Dashboard">
    It's the Hirify home page. Find the **Start an interview** row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the video meeting link">
    In the **Paste a Meet or Teams link** field, paste the meeting address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Press Enter or click the arrow on the right of the field. The message "Meeting assistant invited to the meeting" confirms it's on its way.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the link isn't recognized, Hirify replies "Invalid link. Use a Google Meet or Teams link." and nothing happens.

## Invite notes@bot.hirify.fr to your calendar invitation

This is the simplest path for an interview that's already booked. You add the assistant like any other guest.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Schedule by email window">
    On the **Dashboard**, click **Schedule by email**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the address">
    Click **Copy address**. The address shown is `notes@bot.hirify.fr`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it as a guest">
    In your calendar invitation, paste the address into the guest list, then send the invitation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The assistant joins the video meeting at the time set in the event.

<Note>
  This path needs no calendar connection. It works from any calendar tool, as long as the invitation contains a video meeting link and is sent from the email address of your Hirify account.
</Note>

## Add the meeting assistant to an event from the Calendar

This path requires your calendar to be connected to Hirify. It offers something the other two don't: choosing the notes template right away.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Calendar">
    In the sidebar, under **My workspace**, click **Calendar**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the event">
    A panel opens on the right with the event title and time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a notes template">
    Under **Notes template**, select the one to apply. You can skip this and do it later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add the meeting assistant">
    The message "Meeting assistant added" confirms it's scheduled.
  </Step>
</Steps>

On an event that's already scheduled and hasn't started yet, the same panel offers **Update** to change the template and **Delete** to cancel. Deleting asks for confirmation: "The meeting assistant won't join this meeting anymore. This can't be undone." Once the event has started, the panel only offers **Close**.

When your calendar is connected, the Dashboard also shows an **Agenda** block, split into **Today** and **Upcoming**. Each event without a scheduled assistant has an **Add the assistant** button, which takes you to the **Calendar** to finish.

{/* SCREENSHOT: the side panel of a Calendar event, with the template picker and the Add the meeting assistant button */}

## Check that the meeting assistant joined

Two places tell you.

In the Dashboard's **Agenda** block, an event with a scheduled assistant shows **Assistant scheduled** instead of the **Add the assistant** button. On the **Calendar**, it has a small robot icon in the top-right corner of the event tile.

Under **Interviews**, the interview goes through **Pending**, **Assistant joined**, **Meeting in progress**, then **Processing transcript**. The list updates by itself.

<Warning>
  If the meeting screens people before letting them in, the host must admit the assistant like any other participant. Otherwise the interview stops at **Assistant not admitted** and no recording is kept.
</Warning>

## Good to know

- The Dashboard field and its error message mention Google Meet and Teams.
- Participants can see the meeting assistant. Tell them about the recording before you start it. See [Inform your candidates](/en/trust/inform-your-candidates).
- A recording shorter than one minute isn't transcribed. The interview shows **Too short**.
- Once the event has started, you can no longer change the template of a scheduled assistant. On an event that has ended, the panel shows "Notes templates can't be changed once the event has ended."
- The **Calendar** is the only path that lets you choose the notes template when you schedule the assistant. The other two leave that choice for later.
- You can change the image the assistant shows in meetings. See [Customize the meeting assistant](/en/integrations/customize-the-meeting-assistant).
