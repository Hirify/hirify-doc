Hirify tells you in the app about what moves forward without you: an interview transcribed, interview notes generated, a request to join, or an event coming from your ATS. This page shows where to read them and what you can turn off.

## Open the notification center

Click your name at the very bottom of the sidebar, then hover over **Notifications**. The list opens on the right.

A red dot appears on your avatar and next to **Notifications** as soon as something is unread. The list itself loads when you open the panel.

## Filter by type

Three tabs filter the list: **All**, **Transcripts**, and **Notes**. Each one shows the number of unread items it covers.

When nothing matches, the panel shows "No notifications" and "Your notifications will show up here".

## What Hirify notifies you about

The notifications seen in the app:

| Notification | When it arrives |
| --- | --- |
| **Transcript ready** | An interview's transcript is available |
| **Transcript failed** | The transcription didn't go through |
| **Notes ready** | The interview notes are ready |
| **Notes failed** | Generating the interview notes failed |
| **Request to join your organization** | A colleague asks to join |
| **Request approved** / **Request declined** | Your own request to join was handled |
| **ATS fields filled in** | Fields were filled in in your ATS |
| **Pipeline insights** | Points worth your attention were spotted in your pipeline |

Each row shows its title, its message, and how long ago it arrived.

## Open the related item

Click a notification. It's marked as read and takes you to where the action happened. A notification about an interview opens that interview. If it's about interview notes, the interview opens straight on the notes view. A **Pipeline insights** notification opens the matching conversation in the Assistant.

To clear the red dot in one go, use **Mark all as read** at the top of the panel. The button stays inactive when everything is already read.

## Unsubscribe from marketing emails

Hirify has no notification settings. The notifications listed above can't be turned on or off.

The only emails you can stop are marketing emails.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the unsubscribe link">
    It's at the bottom of the marketing emails Hirify sends. The page is called **Email preferences** and shows the account concerned.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Unsubscribe from marketing emails**. The **Cancel** button closes the page without changing anything.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Unsubscribing only affects marketing emails. You'll keep receiving the messages tied to your account: security notifications, transaction confirmations, and other emails the service needs to send.
</Note>

## Good to know

- The unsubscribe link is personal and tied to your account. Without it, the page can't be reached.
- If you've already unsubscribed, the page confirms it instead of asking again.
- Notifications are individual. They follow your account, not your organization.
- Nothing can be deleted from the panel. Notifications are marked as read, they aren't erased.
- Your interview list updates by itself while processing runs. You don't need to wait for a notification to see an interview move forward.
