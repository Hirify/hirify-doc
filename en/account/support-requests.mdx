A conversation opened with support leaves a trace in Hirify. The **Support requests** page gathers your organization's requests with their status and latest activity. It helps when several people have written in and nobody knows where things stand.

## Open support requests

Click your name at the very bottom of the sidebar, then **Support requests**. The entry only appears if you can manage the organization's members.

Just above it in the same menu, **Contact support** opens the support chat. That's where you create a request, not from the tracking page.

## Read the table

A count line opens the page, for example "4 requests · 1 open". Below it, each request takes one row of the table.

| Column | What it contains |
| --- | --- |
| **Status** | **Open** or **Resolved** |
| **Request** | A summary of your request, with the **Support reply** below it when there is one |
| **Category** | How the request is classified, or **Uncategorized** |
| **Requested by** | The person in your organization who made the request |
| **Last activity** | The date of the last message, followed by the number of messages exchanged |

When no request has been opened yet, the page shows "No support requests yet."

## Categories

Requests fall into six categories: **Incident**, **Bug**, **Question**, **Billing**, **Feature request**, and **Other**. A request that just arrived stays **Uncategorized** until it's classified.

You don't pick the category when you write to support. It's inferred from the conversation and updates as the exchange goes on.

## Good to know

- The page is read-only. You can't reply to a request or reopen it there. Pick up the conversation in the support chat instead.
- The table shows a summary of the request and the reply, never the full conversation.
- Tracking works at the organization level. You also see your colleagues' requests, not just your own.
- Access is limited to administrators and billing admins. Members and managers don't have this entry in their menu.
- A resolved request stays available. Its status changes, but it stays in the table.
