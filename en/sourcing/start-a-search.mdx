Sourcing finds the profiles in your own talent pool who come closest to an open job. You describe the need in a single text field. Hirify goes through your analyzed interviews and the profiles in your ATS, then assesses each profile criterion by criterion. If you connected your LinkedIn account, the search can read LinkedIn too.

## Write the brief

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Sourcing">
    In the sidebar, under **Sourcing**, click **Searches**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type or paste">
    In the **Describe the profile you're looking for or paste a job description…** field, write what you need in one sentence or paste a full job ad. Next to the send button, a menu sets the search's [depth](#choose-the-depth). When LinkedIn is available to your organization, a second menu picks [where to search](#choose-where-to-search).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check what Hirify understood">
    As soon as you stop typing, Hirify reads the brief and shows what it understood under the field. See [What Hirify reads in your brief](#what-hirify-reads-in-your-brief).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Press ⌘ or Ctrl+Enter, or click the arrow button. Enter on its own inserts a line break, so you can paste a job description without starting the search. Under 20 characters, you can't send, unless a job is attached.
  </Step>
</Steps>

At the top of the screen, a counter shows the sourcing credits you have left this month. If your LinkedIn account is connected, a second counter shows the LinkedIn results left today.

## Choose the depth

The **Depth** menu, next to the send button, sets how many profiles the search aims for and what it costs in sourcing credits:

- **Quick**: about 5 qualified profiles, for 1 credit.
- **Standard**: about 10 qualified profiles, for 2 credits. It's marked **Recommended**.
- **Deep**: about 20 qualified profiles, for 4 credits.

The first time, the depth is **Standard**. After that, your choice is remembered in this browser.

Sourcing credits are your organization's monthly credits. A depth your remaining credits don't cover is grayed out. If they no longer cover the depth you picked, the menu switches to the deepest one they still cover. Credits are only taken once a profile has been evaluated: "A search that fails, that you stop or that finds no profile to evaluate costs nothing."

When LinkedIn is one of the sources, each depth also shows how many LinkedIn results it reads: 25, 50, or 100. If your LinkedIn account has fewer left today, the line says so, for example "LinkedIn: … of 50 results, daily limit".

## Choose where to search

When LinkedIn is available to your organization, a second menu next to the send button picks the sources. Its button names the active ones: your ATS's name, or **Talent pool** when no ATS is connected, followed by **LinkedIn** when it's on.

The **Search in** menu has two rows, each with its own switch:

- **My talent pool**: your candidates and analyzed interviews.
- **LinkedIn**: a search run through your LinkedIn Recruiter or Sales Navigator account.

One source always stays on. You can search LinkedIn alone, but you can't turn both off. Your sources are remembered in this browser for your next searches.

Without a LinkedIn account that is connected and able to search, the LinkedIn row shows "Account not connected" and a **Connect** button, which opens **Settings**, **Integrations**. The connection goes through [the Hirify extension](/en/integrations/chrome-extension).

With LinkedIn on, the menu adds:

- **Hide people already viewed or contacted**: leaves out the people you already viewed or contacted on LinkedIn and those your organization already evaluated or contacted. It's on by default, and your choice is remembered in this browser.
- **A gauge**: "This search takes up to … of the … results left today", with a **+0 credit** tag. LinkedIn draws on your account's daily limit, never on your sourcing credits.
- **A reminder**: the search goes through your LinkedIn session. Check the contract it uses there, and don't run anything else in it while the search runs.

What the LinkedIn search does:

- **People already in your ATS** are always left out. The progress line gives their number.
- **If it finds too few people**, it searches again without its narrowest filters. The progress line says so, for example "search area widened" or "with no filter on industry".
- **After the search**, Hirify reads some LinkedIn profiles in full, at your account's pace: "Reading full LinkedIn profiles · … of …".

## What Hirify reads in your brief

Under the field, four chips show what Hirify read in your text:

- **Location**
- **Languages**
- **Compensation**
- **Contract & start**

A checked chip found its information. Click it to see the value Hirify kept, where it came from (**Read in your text**, with the excerpt, or **From the attached job.**), and its effect on the search. To change the value, edit the text.

A dotted chip flags a point missing from the brief. Click it to add its line to your text, for example "Location: ", then fill it in. A missing point never stops you from starting the search.

If the reading fails, the message **Reading unavailable, the search still runs.** appears. You can start the search anyway.

## Filter or criterion

A pay, contract, or availability condition written as a requirement, such as "day rate 650 € max", becomes a **Filter**. Its chip then shows the **Filter** tag.

- **A filter** rules out, before evaluation, the profiles whose file contradicts the condition. A profile whose information is unknown is never ruled out.
- **A criterion** never removes a profile from the list. At the **Must-have** level, a contradicted criterion puts the profile in the **Ruled out** group, which you can still open. At the **Important** or **Bonus** level, it only affects the order.

To turn a filter into a simple preference, open its chip and click **Relax into a criterion**. The criterion then starts at the **Important** level. See [Criterion levels](/en/sourcing/weight-the-criteria).

Location and languages are never filters. They become criteria of the search: languages at **Must-have**, and location at **Must-have** when the job requires being there in person.

## Attach a job from your ATS

If your ATS is connected, type **@** in the field, or click the **@** button (**Mention a job**). The list of your jobs opens, with their department, location, and reference. Type a title or a reference to filter it, then pick a job. It's inserted into the text.

- **One job per search**: attaching another one replaces the first.
- **The job description completes the brief**: with a job attached, the text can stay empty. Add what the job description doesn't say, such as a location constraint.
- **A reference quoted in the text**: if your brief mentions a job's reference, Hirify offers **Attach “…”**. **Dismiss the suggestion** removes it.

Starting from a real job has another effect: a profile who already applied to that job shows **Applied before** in the results.

## Who will see the search

A search is visible to your whole team by default. If your brief asks for a confidential search, for example "confidential assignment", the **Private search** chip appears and only you will see the search. The search's author can change this setting later from **Share**. See [Read the results](/en/sourcing/read-and-compare#share-the-search).

## Follow the search's progress

At the top right of the results, a line tracks the search: **Searching the talent pool**, then **Evaluating** with the number of profiles evaluated out of the number found. Profiles appear as they're evaluated. The **Stop** button interrupts the search. Evaluations already started still finish.

Once the search is done, the line shows the number of profiles evaluated and how long ago, then, when relevant, how many profiles were filtered out and by which filter.

<Note>
  You can close the tab. The search keeps running, and you'll find it in the **Pick up where you left off** list, under the field on the Sourcing home screen.
</Note>

Only one search runs at a time. If you start one while another is running, a panel tells you so, with **View that search** and **Stop the other and launch**. At the top of the screen, **1 search running** takes you back to the one in progress.

## When LinkedIn doesn't go through

If LinkedIn can't be read, the search carries on without it. The progress line shows "Search ran without LinkedIn:" followed by the reason:

- "account unavailable"
- "daily quota reached": your LinkedIn account used up its daily limit.
- "LinkedIn slowed requests down"
- "feature turned off": LinkedIn is switched off on Hirify's side for now.
- "LinkedIn did not respond"
- "LinkedIn session in use elsewhere, possibly open in another tab": the line adds "Close LinkedIn in your other tabs, then run the search again."
- "timed out"
- "search stopped": you stopped the search.
- "the read waited too long"

A LinkedIn-only search doesn't look at your talent pool. If LinkedIn doesn't go through, the screen shows **The LinkedIn search did not go through**, with the reason. If LinkedIn returns no one, it shows **No profile found on LinkedIn**. Either way, it adds "Your talent pool was not searched."

## Run again or start from a new brief

To change the brief, start a new search: click **Sourcing** in the breadcrumb or in the sidebar to go back to the input field. To change only a criterion's level, there's no need to run the search again. See [Criterion levels](/en/sourcing/weight-the-criteria).

Other buttons start the search again depending on the situation:

- **Run search again**: when no profile could be evaluated, or for a search run before the switch to criteria.
- **Run again without filters**: when your filters ruled out every profile found.
- **Resume**: after a search you stopped.
- **Evaluate**: when profiles were found but not yet evaluated.
- **New search**: to start again from a blank page.

<Warning>
  Each run uses one search from your hourly budget, which is capped at ten. Changing a criterion's level uses none.
</Warning>

## Who can use Sourcing (from the Hub plan)

Sourcing is available from the Hub plan. On a Starter plan, the **Sourcing** entry stays visible in the sidebar but is grayed out and inactive. Its tooltip says "Available on the Teams plan", which refers to the Hub plan.

It also needs material to work on. If your talent pool doesn't contain any indexed profile yet, a **Your talent pool isn't indexed yet** panel appears, with a **Connect ATS** button. Run a first interview or [connect your ATS](/en/integrations/connect-your-ats) to feed the database.

## Good to know

- **Sourcing searches your talent pool, and LinkedIn if you add it.** It doesn't browse the web. It only reads your analyzed interviews, the candidates in your ATS, and LinkedIn through your own account when you turn it on.
- **Ten searches per hour, per user.** Once the quota is reached, the screen shows how long until the next one. The Assistant stays available, and **Switch to Assistant** takes you there.
- **The target depends on the depth**: about 5, 10, or 20 qualified profiles. The search stops when it has found them, when it has gone through everything your talent pool contains, or when its time runs out. In that last case, it shows the best profiles it got.
- **A profile with no analyzed interview can still be evaluated** on its CV and ATS record. A criterion these documents don't cover is marked **Nothing on file**, which isn't a bad mark.
- **Nothing is sent to anyone.** A search produces a list, not a contact.
