Before you connect an ATS or capture your first interview, you need a fully configured Hirify account. This page covers every step from the initial sign-up form through selecting the right plan and personalizing your profile so the platform is ready for your team from day one.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up at app.hirify.fr">
    Open [app.hirify.fr](https://app.hirify.fr) in your browser and click **Get Started**. You can register in one of two ways:

    - **Email and password** — available on all plans. Enter your work email address, choose a strong password, and click **Create account**.
    - **Single Sign-On (SSO)** — available exclusively on the **Volume** plan. Your IT administrator provisions access through your organization's identity provider (Okta, Azure AD, and Google Workspace are supported). If your company uses SSO, contact [support@hirify.fr](mailto:support@hirify.fr) before signing up so your account is linked to the correct directory.

    <Note>
      Volume plan customers receive a guided onboarding session with a Hirify implementation specialist, including SSO configuration, bulk seat provisioning, and a tailored template library built from your most common job descriptions.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify your email address">
    After submitting the sign-up form, Hirify sends a verification email to the address you provided. Open the email and click **Confirm my email**. The link expires after 24 hours — if it lapses, return to the login page and click **Resend verification email**.

    You cannot access your workspace until your email address is verified. Check your spam folder if the message does not arrive within two minutes.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete the onboarding wizard">
    Once verified, Hirify launches a short onboarding wizard. Provide the following details:

    - **Company name** — used on exports, shared templates, and invoices.
    - **Team size** — helps Hirify recommend the right plan and pre-load relevant templates.
    - **Primary ATS** — select the applicant tracking system your team uses today. You can connect it immediately or skip and connect later from **Settings → Integrations**.

    The wizard typically takes under three minutes to complete and can be revisited at any time from **Settings → Workspace**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose your plan">
    Select the subscription tier that matches your team's needs. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time from **Settings → Billing**.

    | Plan | Price | Best for |
    |---|---|---|
    | **Starter** | €24 / month | Individual recruiters or small teams exploring AI-assisted hiring |
    | **Hub** | €49 / month | Growing teams that need two-way ATS sync and advanced template access |
    | **Volume** | Custom | Agencies and ESNs with 50+ seats, multi-team rollout, or enterprise SSO requirements |

    All plans include a 14-day free trial — no credit card required to start. At the end of the trial period, you are prompted to enter payment details or your workspace moves to read-only mode until a plan is activated.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set up your profile">
    Navigate to **Settings → My Profile** to finalize your personal details:

    - **Full name and role** — displayed on interview reports and candidate summaries shared with hiring managers.
    - **Notification preferences** — choose whether to receive email alerts for completed transcripts, new candidate matches, and ATS sync events.
    - **Default template** — select the qualification framework Hirify applies automatically when you start a new interview recording.

    Your profile settings are personal and do not affect other members of your workspace.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once your account is set up, invite your colleagues so they can start capturing interviews and searching the shared talent pool right away.
