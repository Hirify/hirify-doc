By connecting directly to your existing ATS, Hirify captures every interview, enriches candidate profiles automatically, and lets you search your entire talent pool in plain language — without changing your workflow or migrating your data.

## Why Hirify?

On average, **80% of a recruitment team's talent pool sits unused**. Candidates are interviewed, qualified, and then forgotten — buried in an ATS that offers no way to search by skills, mobility, or salary expectations. The next time a similar role opens, recruiters start from scratch.

Hirify solves this by turning every interview into structured, searchable data. After each conversation, candidate profiles are automatically enriched with the skills assessed, mobility preferences, salary expectations, and availability — all written back to your ATS in real time.

## Getting Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Account">
    Sign up at [app.hirify.fr](https://app.hirify.fr) and choose the plan that fits your team. See [Account Setup](/getting-started/account-setup) for details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Your ATS">
    Link Hirify to your existing ATS in a few clicks. Hirify reads your current talent pool and begins enriching profiles automatically. See [Connecting Your ATS](/integrations/connecting-your-ats).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run Your First Search">
    Open Talent Pool Chat and ask a question in plain language. Hirify surfaces matching candidates with evidence from their interviews. See [Talent Pool Chat](/features/talent-pool-chat).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite Your Team">
    Add your recruiters so everyone works from the same enriched talent pool. See [Invite Your Team](/getting-started/invite-team).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  All data is hosted in France on ISO 27001-certified infrastructure. Hirify is fully GDPR and AI Act compliant — no candidate data ever leaves the controlled HR perimeter.
</Note>
