## ROI Example

Choosing the right plan isn't just about features — it's about measurable impact on your team's output. Consider a five-recruiter agency where each recruiter conducts 12 interviews per week. Hirify saves approximately 30 minutes per interview in note-taking, structuring, and profile enrichment. That's **30 hours of recruiter time saved every week**, or roughly **€5,800 recovered in productivity per month** — well above the cost of any Hirify plan.

As your team scales, the Hub plan compounds those gains further by syncing all enriched profiles back to your ATS automatically and enabling natural-language search across your entire talent pool.
