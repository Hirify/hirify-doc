Manage your Hirify subscription, view invoices, update payment methods, and change your plan from **Settings** → **Billing**. All billing actions are available to workspace Admins and take effect immediately or at the next billing cycle depending on the change type.

## Viewing Your Current Plan

To see your active plan, seat count, and next renewal date, navigate to **Settings** → **Billing**. The top of the page displays your current plan name, the number of active seats, your billing interval (monthly or annual), and the date of your next charge. A usage summary shows interviews captured and profiles enriched in the current period.

## Upgrading or Downgrading

You can change your plan at any time from **Settings** → **Billing**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the plan selector">
    Go to **Settings** → **Billing** and click **Change Plan**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your new plan">
    Review the available plans — Starter, Hub, or Volume — and select the one that fits your team's current needs. A side-by-side feature comparison is shown to help you decide.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the change">
    Click **Confirm**. Upgrades take effect immediately and you are charged a prorated amount for the remainder of the current billing period. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle, so you retain full access to your current plan's features until then.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Downgrading from Hub to Starter disables Talent Pool Chat and ATS integration at the start of the next billing cycle. Your existing enriched candidate profile data is fully preserved — nothing is deleted. You can upgrade again at any time to restore access.
</Note>

## Adding Seats

To add seats to your current plan, go to **Settings** → **Billing** → **Add Seats**, enter the number of additional seats you need, and confirm. New seats are charged on a prorated basis for the remainder of the current billing period, and the full seat price applies from the next cycle onward.

<Tip>
  If you are growing quickly, consider switching to the Volume plan (50+ seats) for custom pricing and a dedicated account setup. See the [Plans](/account/plans) page for a full comparison.
</Tip>

## Payment Methods

Hirify accepts all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express). To update your payment method, go to **Settings** → **Billing** → **Payment Method** and enter your new card details. Changes apply to the next scheduled charge.

Invoices are generated automatically after each billing cycle and sent to the billing email address on file. You can update the billing email in **Settings** → **Billing** → **Billing Details**.

## Invoices

All past invoices are available for download from **Settings** → **Billing** → **Invoice History**. Each invoice lists the plan, seat count, billing period, and amount charged excl. VAT. Click the **Download PDF** button next to any invoice to save a copy for your records or accounting team.

## Cancellation

To cancel your subscription, go to **Settings** → **Billing** → **Cancel Subscription** and follow the confirmation steps. Cancellation stops future charges and your plan remains active until the end of the current billing period.

<Warning>
  Cancelling your subscription will permanently delete all candidate data — interviews, enriched profiles, and templates — 30 days after the cancellation date. **Export your data before cancelling.** Go to **Settings** → **Data Export** to download a full archive of your workspace.
</Warning>

After cancellation, you have a 30-day window to reactivate your subscription and recover your data by contacting [support@hirify.fr](mailto:support@hirify.fr). Once the 30-day period expires, deletion is irreversible.

<Info>
  Volume plan billing is managed directly by the Hirify team and is not editable through the self-serve billing portal. Contact [support@hirify.fr](mailto:support@hirify.fr) to adjust seat counts, update payment details, or request changes to your contract.
</Info>
