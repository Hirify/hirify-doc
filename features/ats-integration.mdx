Hirify connects to your existing ATS with a two-way integration — it reads your current talent pool and writes enriched candidate data back after every interview. No migration, no double-entry, no change to your recruiter workflows. Your ATS remains your system of record; Hirify simply makes it smarter.

## What the Integration Does

Hirify's bidirectional sync works in both directions simultaneously, keeping your ATS and Hirify perfectly aligned at all times.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Reads from Your ATS" icon="arrow-down-to-line" href="/integrations/ats-overview">
    Hirify imports all your existing candidate profiles into its search index on first connection, so your entire talent pool is immediately available for natural-language queries and enrichment — no manual import required.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Writes Back to Your ATS" icon="arrow-up-from-line" href="/integrations/ats-overview">
    After each interview, Hirify pushes enriched fields — skills, mobility preferences, salary expectations, and availability — directly back to the candidate's profile in your ATS.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Real-Time Synchronisation

Changes flow in both directions continuously. When a recruiter updates a candidate's status in your ATS, Hirify reflects that change immediately. When Hirify captures new information from an interview, it surfaces in your ATS without any manual step. There is no nightly batch job, no export-import cycle, and no risk of stale data across systems.

## Human Control

Automation handles the heavy lifting, but every write is gated by a human decision.

<Note>
  Hirify never makes automatic writes without recruiter approval. Every profile update is reviewed before it is pushed to the ATS. You stay in control of what goes into your system of record — Hirify surfaces the enriched data and waits for your confirmation before committing it.
</Note>

This approval flow is designed to meet GDPR requirements and internal data-quality standards. You can review pending updates individually or in bulk from the **Review Queue** in the Hirify dashboard.

## Setting Up the Integration

Connecting your ATS takes a few minutes and requires no technical expertise. The process follows the same pattern for all supported platforms:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integration Settings">
    In the Hirify app at [app.hirify.fr](https://app.hirify.fr), navigate to **Settings → Integrations** and select your ATS from the list of supported platforms.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorise the Connection">
    Follow the OAuth or API-key flow for your specific ATS. Hirify guides you through each step with platform-specific instructions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Sync Preferences">
    Choose which candidate fields to import, which fields Hirify is allowed to write back, and whether enrichment updates require manual approval or can be auto-queued for review.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the Initial Import">
    Trigger the first-time sync to load your existing talent pool into Hirify's search index. Depending on the size of your database, this typically completes within a few minutes.
  </Step>
</Steps>
