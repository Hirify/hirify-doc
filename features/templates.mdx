Templates define the qualification criteria for a specific role. Hirify generates a template from your job description, giving every interviewer on your team the same structured framework — so candidate evaluations are consistent, comparable, and auditable. Whether you are filling one position or running a high-volume campaign, templates eliminate the guesswork and subjectivity that creep into unstructured interviews.

## Creating a Template

You can generate a new qualification framework in under two minutes by pasting your job description directly into Hirify.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Templates">
    In the Hirify app at [app.hirify.fr](https://app.hirify.fr), select **Templates** from the main navigation. You will see your team's existing templates alongside the pre-built library.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'New Template'">
    Select **New Template** in the top-right corner to open the template builder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste Your Job Description">
    Paste your full job description into the input field — the more detail you provide, the more precise the generated criteria will be. You can also type a description from scratch if you do not have an existing posting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate Qualification Criteria">
    Click **Generate**. Hirify's AI analyses the job description and produces a structured set of qualification criteria organised by category — technical skills, soft skills, experience level, mobility, and compensation range.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and Adjust">
    Read through the generated criteria and make any adjustments. You can add criteria, remove irrelevant ones, reorder categories, and change the weighting of each criterion to reflect your client's priorities.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and Share with Your Team">
    Click **Save**. The template is immediately available to all team members and will appear in the template picker whenever anyone starts a new interview for a matching role.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Applying a Template to an Interview

Before starting an interview, Hirify prompts you to select a template for the role you are filling. Once selected, the template works silently in the background during the conversation.

As the interview progresses, Hirify maps what the candidate says to each qualification criterion and builds a structured score in real time. At the end of the session, you receive a completed evaluation card with a criterion-by-criterion breakdown — no manual note-taking or post-interview scoring required.

<Accordion title="What happens if I start an interview without selecting a template?">
  Hirify still captures and structures the interview, but the output will not be scored against a specific role's criteria. You can apply a template retroactively from the interview record — Hirify will re-analyse the transcript and produce a scored evaluation card after the fact.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I switch templates mid-interview?">
  Templates are locked once an interview begins to ensure evaluation integrity. If you realise you have selected the wrong template, end the session, select the correct template, and restart. The initial session is saved and can be discarded or kept for reference.
</Accordion>

## Sharing Templates

Once you save a template, it is immediately visible to everyone on your team. There is no publish step, no permission to grant — saved templates are shared by default.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Starter & Hub Plans">
    All templates are shared across your entire workspace. Any team member can use, duplicate, or edit a template. Edits are versioned so you can always revert to a previous state.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Volume Plan">
    Volume plan customers can create segment-specific templates and assign them to individual teams or client accounts. A template created for one team is not visible to others unless explicitly shared, giving large agencies full control over their qualification frameworks across multiple business units.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Tip>
  Templates are especially powerful for high-volume roles. Create one template per job family — for example, a single "Software Developer" template that covers all mid-to-senior developer positions — and reuse it across your entire team. Every evaluation then uses the same criteria, making candidates directly comparable and giving you an auditable, GDPR-compliant record of every hiring decision.
</Tip>
