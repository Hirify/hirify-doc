After every interview captured by Hirify, structured data is written directly back to the candidate's profile in your ATS. There is no manual entry, no copy-pasting from notes, and no lost information. Every qualification signal extracted from the conversation — skills, mobility preferences, salary expectations, availability — is stored in a structured, searchable format against the candidate record, instantly available to every recruiter on your team.

## Profile Fields Enriched

Hirify enriches candidate profiles with a consistent set of structured fields after each interview. The table below describes what is captured and how it is stored.

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Skills assessed** | Technical skills (tools, languages, certifications) and soft skills (communication, leadership, adaptability), extracted from the conversation |
| **Mobility** | Preferred cities and regions, remote / hybrid / on-site preferences, willingness to relocate |
| **Salary expectations** | Declared salary range and currency, including any distinction between fixed and variable components |
| **Availability** | Earliest start date, notice period length, and any constraints mentioned by the candidate |
| **Interview summary** | A concise AI-generated overview of the candidate's experience, profile, and key strengths |
| **Qualification score** | A per-criteria score aligned to the qualification template applied before the interview (where applicable) |

## Explainable AI

Every field enriched by Hirify is backed by evidence from the interview transcript. When you open a candidate profile, you can click on any enriched data point — a skill, a salary figure, a mobility preference — to see the exact quote from the conversation that generated it. There is no black-box scoring and no unexplained recommendations.

This explainability principle applies to qualification scores as well. If a template was applied and the candidate received a score against a specific criterion, you can trace that score back to the precise moment in the interview transcript where the evidence was found. This makes Hirify's assessments auditable and defensible — important for both internal quality control and GDPR accountability requirements.

## Profile History

Candidate profiles in Hirify accumulate structured data across multiple interviews over time. If a candidate is interviewed more than once — for different roles, or at different stages of the same process — each enrichment is stored as a separate, timestamped record attributed to the interviewer who conducted that session.

This means you can see how a candidate's profile has evolved: new skills surfaced in a later interview, a change in salary expectations, or updated availability information. The full history is preserved, and earlier records are never overwritten. Your talent pool becomes richer with every interaction, building a longitudinal picture of each candidate that a standard ATS alone cannot provide.
